Methods (no third-party code): src/effects/islamic-stars.js follows
E. H. Hankin's polygons-in-contact method as described by Craig S. Kaplan,
"Islamic Star Patterns from Polygons in Contact", Graphics Interface 2005;
src/effects/domain-colouring.js and analytic-field-lab.js follow the domain
colouring method credited to Frank Farris; src/effects/black-hole.js follows Otto Seiskari's photon
orbit equation (oseiskar.github.io/black-hole); src/effects/fractal-flame.js,
fractal-flame-lab.js, fractal-flame-volume-flight.js, flam3-density-lab.js and
src/workers/progressive-density-worker.js use the variation functions of
Scott Draves and Erik Reckase's fractal flame algorithm;
src/effects/mandelbulb-slice.js, mandelbulb-flight.js and
triplex-mutation-flight.js are the Mandelbulb of Daniel White and Paul
Nylander; src/effects/burning-ship.js is the fractal of Michael Michelitsch
and Otto E. Roessler; src/effects/lyapunov-fractal.js follows Mario Markus;
src/effects/buddhabrot-lab.js follows Melinda Green's Buddhabrot technique
(superliminal.com/fractals/bbrot); src/effects/phyllotaxis.js,
lsystem-tube-flight.js and ifs-lsystem-lab.js follow Prusinkiewicz
and Lindenmayer, "The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants"; every raymarched scene
uses John C. Hart's sphere tracing; the 3D flights follow Mikael Hvidtfeldt
Christensen's (Syntopia) distance-estimated 3D fractals series;
mandelbox-flight.js, folded-box-variants-flight.js and
path-traced-fractal-flight.js are Tom Lowe's Mandelbox; kifs-flight.js,
polyfold-flight.js, difs-tunnel-flight.js, endless-scenes.js and
robot-foundry.js use
kaleidoscopic IFS folds and Menger constructions (Tim McGraw; Paul Bourke);
several flight modes follow Fractal Forums formulas as collected in
Fragmentarium and Mandelbulber; quaternion-julia-flight.js and
hypercomplex-slice-flight.js follow Keenan Crane, "Ray Tracing Quaternion
Julia Sets on the GPU"; strange-attractor-flight.js and lorenz-attractor.js
follow Edward Lorenz (1963) and J. C. Sprott (1994), as does
attractor-density-lab.js with the other classic flows and maps its modes
are named after; endless-sky-crystal.js
uses Rayleigh and Mie
scattering after Sean O'Neil, GPU Gems 2; fluid-ink.js follows Jos Stam,
"Stable Fluids" (SIGGRAPH 1999) and Mark J. Harris, "Fast Fluid Dynamics
Simulation on the GPU" (GPU Gems); lenia.js follows Stephan Rafler's
SmoothLife and Bert Wang-Chak Chan's Lenia; physarum.js follows Jeff Jones's
Physarum agent model (Artificial Life 16(2), 2010); reaction-diffusion.js is
the Gray-Scott model as in Karl Sims's Reaction-Diffusion Tutorial;
curl-silk.js moves
its particles along curl noise (Robert Bridson and colleagues, SIGGRAPH
2007).
